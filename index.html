Warranty & Accessories — Terms & Conditions

1. Eligibility & Lifetime Warranty

Applies to the original registered owner of a new Zyntra vehicle bought from an authorised dealer in India. Covers manufacturing defects in the electric motor, motor controller and body structure. It is generally non-transferable; a change of ownership needs an authorised inspection and Zyntra’s written approval.

2. Battery Warranty

8 years or 1,60,000 km, whichever comes first. If battery State of Health falls below 70% in this period, it will be repaired or replaced. Damage from physical impact, submersion beyond rated limits, unauthorised repair or modification, and use of non-approved chargers is not covered.

3. Service Requirement

To keep the warranty valid, the vehicle must be serviced at an authorised Zyntra service centre as per schedule (every 10,000 km or 12 months, whichever comes first). Missed services may affect the related coverage.

4. Exclusions

Wear-and-tear parts such as tyres, brake pads, wipers, bulbs and the 12V battery; damage from accidents, floods, fire or natural disasters; misuse or racing; undeclared commercial use; and unauthorised modifications are not covered.

5. Accessories

Genuine Zyntra accessories carry the warranty period stated on the invoice (2 years for the wall-box charger, generally 1 year for others). Wall-box installation must be done by an authorised technician and needs an earthed supply and a suitable MCB. Damage caused by non-genuine accessories is not covered.

6. Claims & General Terms

Raise claims through the Zyntra app or helpline. After inspection, Zyntra will decide whether to repair or replace. Prices, features and government subsidies may change without notice, and images are for illustration. All disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of the courts in Jaipur, Rajasthan.

*Terms and conditions apply. This is a summary; the warranty booklet provided at vehicle purchase prevails.
